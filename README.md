# lubelogger-ja-jp

Japanese translation (ja_JP) for LubeLogger, localized for Japanese car and motorcycle users.

---

## Overview

This repository provides a Japanese translation file (`ja_JP.json`) for LubeLogger.

This translation is **not a literal word-for-word translation**.  
It focuses on localization for real-world usage in Japan, especially for private car and motorcycle owners.

The primary goals are usability, clarity, and cultural alignment rather than strict fidelity to the original English text.

---

## Translation Policy

This translation follows these principles:

- Prioritizes terminology commonly used by Japanese car and motorcycle users
- Focuses on practical usability over literal translation
- Avoids forced translation of concepts that are uncommon or unclear in Japan
- Maintains functional consistency without breaking application behavior
- Prefers leaving original text rather than introducing misleading translations

As a result, some labels may differ significantly from the original English wording,  
but application usage remains consistent and non-breaking.

---

## Intentionally Untranslated Areas

Some areas are intentionally left untranslated or only partially translated:

- Tabs that are rarely used by typical Japanese private vehicle owners, such as `Equipment` and `Inspections`
- Advanced server and system configuration settings
- Features that do not have a commonly understood equivalent in Japanese vehicle culture, such as `Vehicle Map`

These areas are intentionally left in their original English (`en_US`) form rather than introducing translations that could be inaccurate or misleading.

---

## Target Users

- Individual users in Japan
- Car and motorcycle owners managing maintenance records
- Users tracking:
  - Odometer-based maintenance
  - Legal inspection cycles
  - Insurance and tax reminders
  - Long-term vehicle ownership

## Installation

1. Download `ja_JP.json` from this repository.
2. Open **LubeLogger** and go to **Settings**.
3. Open the **Translation** section and upload `ja_JP.json`.
4. Select Japanese (`ja_JP`) as the language.

Depending on your LubeLogger version, the exact menu names or locations may differ.
